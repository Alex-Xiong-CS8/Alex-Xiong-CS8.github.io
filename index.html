My name is Alex Xiong and I am a 2nd year Theater, Film, and Digital Production major at the University of California Riverside. I was born and raised in Temecula, California, located in the Inland Empire. It is about 45 minutes to and from UCR. It is known for its championship golf courses and the winery where people often visit for taking fancy photos and tasting wine. I am of Hmong descent as my parents, aunts, uncles, and grandparents have immigrated from Laos, a country in Southeast Asia. I am the middle child of my family as I have one older brother and one younger sister. Throughout my life, I have attended Pauba Valley Elementary School, Vail Ranch Middle School, and Great Oak High School. Growing up, I was very adventurous and curious about the world around me, traveling in Temecula, San Diego, Sacramento, and many other parts of California. As a kid, I have been very passionate about watching different types of movies and tv shows like Toy Story. I have also been very passionate about cosplaying as I love creating costumes based on various characters from different types of media such as anime, cartoons, tv shows, video games, etc. My favorite characters I love cosplaying are Ash Ketchum (Pokémon), Saiki K (The Disastrous Life of Saiki K), Goku (Dragon Ball), and Monkey D. Luffy (One Piece). Sometimes, once in a while, I also enjoy attending UCR sports games such as the Basketball games out of fun and wanting to dance in the crowd to bring spirit. At times, my buddy Arthur and I would constantly be seen together dancing on the dance cam projected on the big screen during timeouts. Along with the exciting events that I have attended here, there are future opportunities that are waiting for me to pursue.

Back in Great Oak High School, I have taken 3 years of CTE Media Arts, aka Video Media Arts. In August 2021, I took Intro to Video Media Arts where I learned about the three basic phases of production. The first phase is pre-production, the process of scriptwriting and storyboarding, both of which help the filmmakers plan out different types of shots, angles, names, and settings. The second phase is production, which involves filming, scouting locations, and directing our actors. Last, the third and final phase is post-production, the phase of editing footage together with special effects, audio, graphics, and transitions. Two years later, I took Advanced Video Media Arts, aka Media Arts 2. THis class was very similar to the intro class, but this time, we were responsible for creating different types of episodes for our school broadcast, Wolfpack TV. Reaching my senior year, I finally took Capstone Media Arts, aka Media Arts 3. Here in this class, we the students are assigned to film live sports events that were going on at Great Oak. Ever since I graduated from Great Oak back in June 2025, I committed to the University of California Riverside.

As a student here at the University of California Riverside, I am planning to not only improve on managing my time better with homework and school, but also hanging out with friends and attending college events on campus like sports or gatherings. I am still trying to figure out my major by thinking about opportunities such as the R’Shorts film club and possibly making content for me and for other clubs and events I attend. My goals here at UCR are to graduate on time in 2029 with my BA degree in TFDP, and to network and communicate with professors, staff, and faculty here that will help me pursue different types of opportunities in film.
